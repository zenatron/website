A private relationship dashboard for couples — one shared room with a canvas,
daily questions, a photo gallery, moods, rituals, time capsules and scheduled
notifications.

The whole thing is self-hosted. Not as a feature, as the premise: a shared space
for two people is the last kind of data that should sit on someone else's
analytics pipeline.

## What's in the room

- **A shared canvas** both people draw on, in real time.
- **Daily questions** — one prompt a day, both answers revealed together.
- **Photos**, moods and rituals, kept to the pair.
- **Time capsules** — messages written now and delivered on a date you choose.
- **Scheduled notifications**, so the rituals actually happen.

<Callout type="note" title="Real-time, not polled">
The canvas and presence run over a live connection rather than an interval. That
is the difference between a shared drawing and two people watching a slideshow
of each other's work.
</Callout>

## Running it

Docker Compose with Postgres, and authentication delegated to
[Pocket ID](https://github.com/pocket-id/pocket-id) or any OIDC provider — the
app reads an `OIDC_*` environment contract and nothing provider-specific, so
swapping identity providers is configuration rather than a rewrite.
